Total: 76 illustrations. Stable variant metadata, bilingual names and topic matching: `src/QuestBell.Core/Artwork.cs`. Existing variants 0–3 retain their original meaning.

The app selects display rectangles around the principal sprite shapes so stray neighboring pixels at cell edges do not appear in the UI. No PNG pixels are modified. WPF smoke checks export these rectangles to `screenshots/artwork-regions.json`; `docs/artwork-regions.js` wraps that JSON as `const artworkRegions` for matching offline guide framing. Regenerate that file after changing an atlas. New sprites and originals are saved together in `src/QuestBell/Assets/`; new filenames end in `-friends.png`.
